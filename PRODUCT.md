# Product

## Register

brand

## Users
Vintage clothing lovers, collectors, and second-hand buyers seeking unique, high-quality curated apparel, footwear, and accessories from Rapeepong.

## Product Purpose
To serve as an elegant showcase storefront of curated second-hand items. It outlines real-time stock availability (Available, Reserved, Sold), drives buyers to direct chat channels for final transactions, and allows customers to track order shipping progress securely by phone number lookup.

## Brand Personality
- **Curated & Authentic**: Focus on vintage street culture.
- **Sleek & Bold**: High contrast theme of deep dark navy, white, red, and black.
- **Minimalist & Premium**: Avoid visual clutter, allowing high-quality product images to stand out.

## Anti-references
- Over-decorated dropshipping storefronts.
- Bright neon gradients and typical SaaS pricing grids.
- Comically large rounded cards.

## Design Principles
1. **Showcase Visuals First**: Product photography is the hero. Simple framing with clean 1px borders.
2. **Clear Handshake to Chat**: Purchasing is done strictly through chat. The action to initiate chat must be extremely clear and prominent.
3. **Transparent Order Progress**: Reassure customers by providing a clear, visually progressive timeline for order packaging and shipping.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
- Contrast ratio ≥ 4.5:1 for body and supporting text.
- Full responsive support down to small viewports.
- Adaptive styling respecting user `prefers-reduced-motion` settings.
